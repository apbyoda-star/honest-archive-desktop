How it works

  1. Point your scanner at this folder (Brother ADS-3100: set the scan destination to this folder).
  2. Put the paper in and press the scanner's button. Mixed stacks are fine — bills, letters, statements, all together.
  3. Pages are sent as they arrive and filed together once the scanner has been quiet for a minute — Honest Archive sorts them into documents and folders for you.

The Brother setting that saves one file per page is fine — the pages are put back together in order. Saving into a dated folder inside this one is fine too.

If the internet drops or a page jams, nothing is lost: pages wait here and are sent as soon as Honest Archive can be reached.

Filing with folders

  1. Scans dropped loose in this folder are filed as ordinary parts invoices.
  2. To file by category, first create the category in Honest Archive under Settings → Categories, then make a subfolder here with the same name.
  3. If that category is marked private, scans in its folder go straight to your private area and stay hidden from the rest of your team.
Your folders

    Making a folder does not create a category. A folder that doesn’t match one still uploads, but those scans are held for you to review instead of being filed as ordinary invoices — so a folder you haven’t set up yet can never put private paperwork where everyone can see it. Only folders directly inside this one are used, and processed and failed are reserved.

    Scan file requirements

    1. Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, BMP, PDF and TIFF.
    2. File size must be less than 20 MB.
    3. Image dimensions at least 50 × 50 pixels and at most 10000 × 10000 pixels. PDF pages up to 17 × 17 inches — Legal or A3 and smaller.

    Important: files that don't meet these requirements aren't synced — they stay in the folder (moved to failed) so you can fix and re-drop them.
